## What we built and why

M2 prices every quote of the M1 store. Each (source, quote date, expiry) gets a forward from put-call parity on the market's own mids, after the American early-exercise premium has been taken off them on a binomial lattice. Since the review the premium of each quote is priced at **its own American implied vol**, the vol at which the lattice's American price equals the mid, so the procedure is one self-consistent pass (ADR-0008 §3a, amendment 1); since round 2 only where the price identifies that vol, and at the partner's American IV where it does not (amendment 7). Each out-of-the-money quote gets a Black-76 implied vol from its own de-Americanised mid (§7), with an `iv_flags` mask for the three IV-related flags ADR-0009 reserved for M2, and `in_smile` says which IVs are the smile's nodes (since round 3, not a WIDE quote's). Each expiry's smile is nodes in total variance, read by ADR-0004's interpolation and extrapolation rules plus a calendar constraint that keeps total variance from falling with tenor, in which quoted values beat extrapolated ones (round 2). The early-exercise rules of ADR-0003 (the call closed form and the put lattice with its $0.005 tolerance) are pure functions ready for the engine. The build runs one partition per process, skips partitions whose inputs, code and libraries have not changed, deletes pricing partitions whose quotes are gone only on an unfiltered build of a checked store, refuses a summary or export over partitions priced by older code or from quotes and reference tables that changed since (round 3), and writes the same bytes twice. The app gains a Surface page with real SPY surfaces for four dates, a calm one, two stressed ones and ADR-0008's high-rate reference date, beside the illustrative hero, with a data notice on it and on the Data Quality page.

What a user can now do: run `tools/build_pricing.py` and get, for every one of the 19,022,008 quotes, its log-moneyness, its IV where it has one, its American IV and the premium removed, whether it is in the smile, and why any IV is missing [tests, `tests/test_real_pricing.py`]; and open the app's Surface tab.

| PARITY_OUTLIER on **European-equivalent** mids, the ITM side priced at its partner's OTM IV | Raw mids (ADR-0008 §3 as first written); the ITM quote's own American IV | §3a says the steps of §3 run on European-equivalent mids. | **Partitioned, parallel, incremental** build, one (source, year) per process | One pass over all 19 M quotes | A year is at most 2.6 M rows; a rerun with nothing changed prices nothing [build] | A fingerprint per partition in `pricing_inputs.json`, over its quotes, the reference tables, every volbt module the build imports, the library versions and the store's write settings, with the code fingerprint that priced it; a summary or export only when every partition is current; deletions only on an unfiltered build of a checked store (round 2) |
| `surface_frames.json` for **four labelled dates** | ADR-0001's weekly frames | What the brief asked for; enough to compare regimes at 43 KB | A weekly series is a later, additive change |

## Code walkthrough

Read in this order. Each step names one file and line.

1. `src/volbt/pricing/black76.py:60`. `price`: Black-76 on the forward. `price_bounds` (`:76`) is what the IV solver checks first, and `greeks` (`:97`) gives the spot delta with F/S held fixed and theta with F held fixed. `greeks_usd` (`:118`) is ADR-0006's units.
2. `src/volbt/pricing/iv.py:106`. `implied_vol`, for a whole chain: bounds, then the OTM side (an ITM price is solved as its time value), Newton from the Brenner-Subrahmanyam guess (`:99`) to a tolerance relative to the price and to σ, bisection for every quote Newton cannot be trusted with, and the float64 resolution check (`price_rounding`, `:85`), which counts the rounding an ITM time value inherits (round 2). Fail codes, never exceptions; the codes at `:77-83`.
3. `src/volbt/pricing/lattice.py:75`. `crr`: CRR with escrowed dividends, one lattice per option, all stepped together in place, at σ no lower than `sigma_floor` (`:66`); it returns the up-probability it walked with. `crr_european` (`:129`) is the same lattice's European price as a binomial sum; `early_exercise_premium` (`:158`) is their difference.
4. `src/volbt/pricing/deam.py:78`. `american_iv`: σ_A with A(σ_A) = mid for a whole date's quotes, bracketed in [σ_floor, 5], and the premium A − E at it; a σ_A counts only where the price identifies it (round 2, `SENSITIVITY_MIN` at `:67`); bad inputs fail at once with their own codes; calls with no dividend to come skip the walk.
5. `src/volbt/pricing/dividends.py:92`. `projected_ex_dates` (third Fridays, moved to the session before a holiday; past the last session, NYSE's Good Friday and Juneteenth via `nyse_closed_friday`, `:81`), `projected_amount` (`:124`) and `schedule` (`:149`), each drop at `drop_time` (`:144`).
6. `src/volbt/pricing/forward.py:72`. `parity_forward`: K\*, the median of F_K over the 5 strikes nearest it, the dispersion. `carry_forward` (`:51`) is the fallback.
7. `src/volbt/pricing/chain.py:85`. `price_chain`, one snapshot: raw forwards (`:115`), the one American IV solve and the partner's premium for an unidentified quote, the partner solved then if no earlier step had (round 3, `:140-191`, `solve` at `:162`), the de-Americanised forward or `parity_median5_raw` (`:193`), each OTM quote's IV of mid less premium (`:210`), parity outliers on European-equivalent mids (`:244`), `in_smile` (valid, not WIDE since round 3, OTM, with an IV) and the smiles (`:275`). ## Data flow

One quote, the OptionsDX 2023-06-15 SPY 440 put expiring 2023-07-14 (29 days), followed from the store to the app. Its values were read back from the built tables [report, "data flow: OptionsDX 2023-06-15, SPY 440 put expiring 2023-07-14"].

1. **option_quotes** (`data/store/option_quotes/source=optionsdx/year=2023/`): bid 4.99, ask 5.01, quality_flags 0, SPY close 442.60, vendor IV 0.11758. `price_partition` reads the 2023 partition, 1,944,324 rows.
2. **The snapshot**: the 7,702 quotes of 2023-06-15, grouped by date. Per expiry: tau = 29/365; r = 0.053245, from DGS3MO 5.36 dated 2023-06-14, the last value dated before the quote date (`build.rate_on`); one projected dividend, the June ex-date 2023-06-16 at $1.7555 (June 2022's $1.577 times the trailing year's growth 1.1132; SPY paid $1.638, and the old rule's last paid amount was $1.506), dropping just after today's close.
3. **The forward**: the 2023-07-14 expiry has 104 valid call/put pairs. The raw parity forward is 442.2711 (K\* = 442). The 10 strikes nearest K\*, both sides, each get their American IV and lose the premium at it: F = **442.4433**, 3.89 bp higher. The carry forward is 442.7134, so on this date parity stays 6.10 bp below the carry (ADR-0008's Context measured 12 bp for the raw forward against its own carry, of which the premium explained about 4). The raw F_K slope is −0.0283 per strike (ADR-0008: −0.036 over 436-450). All in `expiry_forwards`.
4. **The IV**: the put is OTM (k = ln(440/442.4433) = −0.0055) and in the smile. Its American IV on the lattice is 0.1237 (`iv_american`); its premium there is $0.1373 (`eep`); the IV of the European-equivalent mid 4.8627 on F is **0.1215** (`iv`), Newton, 3 steps. The Black-76 IV of the raw mid, kept only to measure the correction, is 0.1243 (`iv_raw`). The 443 call next to it: 0.1177. The vendor's IVs at 443 were 0.1206 for the call and 0.1149 for the put, 0.57 vol points apart (ADR-0008, Context).
5. **The smile**: the expiry's 104 `in_smile` OTM IVs become nodes (k, w = σ²τ) in `smile_fits`; its ATM IV is 0.1183, fit_flags 0.
6. **The app**: `tools/export_app_data.py` reads 2023-06-15's smiles on the 11 x 26 grid with `surface_iv` and writes the frame into `app/public/data/surface_frames.json` (30-day ATM IV 0.1183); `parseSurfaceFrames` checks it and the Surface page draws it.

3. **Ex-dates are projected by the third-Friday rule**, the Friday moved to the last session in the week before it when it does not trade; past the last SPY session, NYSE's Good Friday and Juneteenth closures move it to the Thursday (review amendment D4, default taken 2026-10-29).

## Not done in M2

- **A per-expiry rate beyond 180 days.** M2 keeps one DGS3MO rate for every expiry. Beyond 180 days `PARITY_OUTLIER` measures the gap between the lattice's carry forward (DGS3MO and the projected dividends) and the market's parity forward, not quote quality: a deep-ITM quote's premium is priced at its partner's OTM IV on a lattice whose carry runs at DGS3MO, which pins its European-equivalent F_K near that carry forward (round 2, design claim 4). Flagged quotes stay in the smile, and the flag does nothing downstream. ADR-0008 amendment 8 proposes a per-expiry, carry-consistent rate (the round-2 reviewer measured 3,600 flagged quotes falling to 356 of 16,768 over 8 dates, forwards moving by up to +42.5 bp at 949 days, ATM IVs by a median 0.10-0.22 vol points on the dates with a rate gap: ADR-0008's wording; the round-2 reviewer's median ATM-IV shifts on its 8 dates were 0.026 (2012-06-15), 0.202 (2015-06-15), 0.102 (2018-04-16), 0.222 (2020-06-15), 0.187 (2022-05-16), -0.003 (2023-02-15), 0.013 (2018-12-31) and 0.039 (2022-12-30) vol points, so 0.10-0.22 is the range of four dates and the other four lie between -0.003 and 0.039; `q3_carryrate.out` and `q3_carryrate_yearend.out`, the reviewer's measurement, not re-run); it is a draft for the owner. **Until the owner decides, strategies trade expiries of at most 180 days: a constraint for M4.**
- **Deep-ITM mids below their exercise value.** 82,751 of 568,856 deep-ITM call mids beyond 180 days are below S − K ([report], "deep in-the-money call mids below S − K"): no American model prices them. Most fall short by little: a median $0.060 (p90 $0.265); 45,621 by more than $0.05, and 8,563 (1.51% of the pairs) by more than 0.10% of S. They only enter the outlier test.
- **The N = 200 lattice's own error on long, low-σ or high-rate chains.** The procedure is exact when the truth is on its own lattice, but against a truth on N = 2,000 it keeps, on the 28 listed chains, up to 7.73 bp (r 10%, 700 days; 0 flagged in all 28); with the procedure on N = 500 the σ 0.08, 700-day chain goes from −3.762 to −0.538 bp ([report], "the sigma 0.08 chain by the procedure's step count"). A larger N for long expiries is a cost decision, not taken.
- **Butterfly arbitrage** in the piecewise-linear smile is not removed or tested; only calendar arbitrage is constrained. Linear interpolation in w can create butterfly arbitrage between quotes that have none (concept 11).
- **Quoted calendar crossings are resolved by raising.** A longer expiry's quote below a shorter one's at the same k is raised to it, by up to 7.51 vol points on the 250 measured dates (a 20-day node on 2020-07-23, raised by an 18-day quote at the same strike; 41.98 before round 3 took WIDE quotes out of the smile); whether such a crossing is a bad quote or a real one is not judged.
- **The spot x vol shock revaluation** that ADR-0001 lists under `volbt.pricing` is used from M5; not built.
- **ADR-0008 §8's American model marks** (N = 500 at the surface IV, at every |k| from M3) and the engine's use of the early-exercise rules are M3. The functions exist and are tested.
- **Weekly surface frames**, and the DoltHub surfaces, are not exported. The app's validator accepts a DoltHub file and shows its notice, but none is built.
- **The Cboe data's licence.** Cboe's website terms allow one copy for personal non-commercial use, and need Cboe's prior written consent to copy, store, create a derivative work from, publish or distribute it (DATA.md sets out the clause). This repository publishes VIX closes and figures computed from Cboe's VIX, VIX3M, SPX and PUT files; DATA.md lists the 14 files for the owner's review. Not resolved here.
- The Newton guess sends 71-78% of OTM solves to bisection. A better start (an OTM-aware guess) would only make the build faster; the ADR fixes the guess. The American IV solve costs 3 to 4 lattice walks per quote plus one for its identification check.
- **Unresolved ITM prices in the solver-accuracy sweep.** 10,842 of the 200,000 ITM prices are unresolved (row "IV solver accuracy" in "Measured results": 101,965 solved, max 4.59e-8; the old fixed cut left 11,769 unresolved). 3,491 of the 10,842 would pass a looser test, that σ(1 ± 1e-6) brackets the float price.
